# Work-Life Balance Guidelines

Northwind Ltd - Policy owner: People and Culture - Effective 1 January 2026 - Version 2026-01

## 1. Why we have these guidelines

People do their best work when they can also take care of their health, family and interests. These guidelines describe the flexibility Northwind Ltd offers and the limits that protect everybody from overwork. They apply to all employees. Managers are responsible for applying them fairly in their teams.

## 2. Flexible working hours

### 2.1 Flexible start and end

Employees may choose their start time between 7:30 and 10:00, as long as they work their contracted hours and are available during the core collaboration hours from 10:00 to 16:00. The team agrees on the arrangement so that customers and colleagues always have a contact. Changes to the arrangement are agreed with the manager at least 5 working days in advance.

### 2.2 Compressed week

Employees who have completed their probation can ask for a compressed week of 4 days of 10 hours, instead of 5 days of 8 hours. The manager approves the request when the work of the team and the customers are not affected. The arrangement is reviewed after 3 months and then every year.

### 2.3 Part-time and job sharing

Employees may ask to work part-time, from 50 percent to 90 percent of a full-time job. Job sharing, where two employees share one role, is possible when the tasks can be divided well. Requests are answered in writing within 20 working days. A refusal needs a business reason, and the employee may appeal to the head of department.

## 3. The right to disconnect

Work stays within working time. To protect rest time, Northwind Ltd applies these rules:

- Employees are not expected to answer emails, messages or calls after 19:00, at weekends or during leave, except when they have agreed to be on call.
- Managers do not send messages to their team after 19:00 or at weekends. When a message must be written outside working hours, the sender schedules it for the next working day.
- Meetings are held between 9:30 and 17:00 only. Meetings of more than 60 minutes need an agenda and a short break.
- Wednesday afternoon from 13:00 is meeting-free, so that people can concentrate on their own work.
- An employee who is contacted urgently outside working hours receives time off in return, in agreement with the manager.

## 4. On-call duty and workload

Employees in on-call rotas, such as IT operations, are on call for at most 1 week in every 4 weeks. On-call duty is paid with an allowance of 250 USD per week and with time off in return for every call-out outside the on-call hours. Overtime is limited to 8 hours per week and requires prior approval. Employees who work more than 10 hours a day on 3 days in a row talk to the manager about the workload. Nobody may be asked to work more than 48 hours per week on average.

## 5. Taking rest

### 5.1 Annual leave

Everybody takes at least 10 consecutive working days of annual leave once per year. The manager plans cover for the team in advance. Employees do not check email during leave. Leave that cannot be taken because of work needs is discussed with the manager and not lost without a conversation.

### 5.2 Wellbeing day

Every employee receives 1 paid wellbeing day per year, in addition to annual leave. It can be taken on any working day with 2 working days notice, and the employee does not need to give a reason.

### 5.3 Wellness hours

Employees can use up to 3 paid wellness hours per month for exercise, a walk, meditation or a health appointment. The hours are agreed with the team and do not need to be recorded.

## 6. Health and mental wellbeing

- The Employee Assistance Programme (EAP) offers free and confidential counselling for employees and their family members. It is available 24 hours a day at +1 (555) 010-5500 and through the PeopleHub app. Each person can use up to 8 counselling sessions per issue per year.
- Trained mental health first aiders are available in every office. Their names are on the intranet page "Wellbeing".
- Managers receive training every 2 years on how to recognise signs of stress and burnout and how to start a conversation.
- Employees who feel overloaded talk to their manager, the HR helpdesk or the EAP. They will not suffer disadvantages.

## 7. Support for families

### 7.1 Returning from parental leave

Employees who return from maternity, paternity, adoption or parental leave can return in a phased way: for the first 4 weeks they may work 60 percent, 70 percent, 80 percent and 90 percent of their hours, and still receive full pay for the first 2 weeks of this phase. Details are agreed with the manager before the return.

### 7.2 Childcare and school holidays

Northwind Ltd pays a childcare allowance of 150 USD per month for each child up to 6 years of age, up to a maximum of 450 USD per month. During school holidays, employees can use flexible hours and up to 5 days of remote work in addition to the normal remote work arrangement, when the work allows.

### 7.3 Care for relatives

Employees who care for a relative can take up to 5 days of paid carers' leave per year and can ask for flexible hours or reduced hours for a limited time. The Leave Policy has the details.

## 8. Remote and hybrid work

Hybrid work supports a good balance between work and private life. The rules for working from home are in the Remote and Hybrid Work Policy. In short, employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week and work from the office on the team days, Tuesday and Thursday.

## 9. Career breaks and sabbatical

After 7 years of service, employees may take a paid sabbatical of 4 weeks (form HR-130). Unpaid leave of up to 3 months is possible after the probation period. These options are described in the Leave Policy.

## 10. How to ask for flexibility

1. The employee discusses the wish with the manager and explains what is needed.
2. The employee and the manager agree on a trial period, usually 3 months.
3. The agreement is recorded in PeopleHub under My Arrangements.
4. After the trial period the manager and the employee review the arrangement and decide to keep, change or stop it.

If the manager refuses, the manager explains the business reason in writing within 10 working days. The employee may ask the HR helpdesk for a second opinion.
