# Health, Safety and Business Travel Policy

Northwind Ltd - Policy owner: Facilities, Finance and People and Culture - Effective 1 January 2026 - Version 2026-01

## 1. Health and safety at work

### 1.1 Responsibility

Northwind Ltd provides a safe and healthy workplace. Managers are responsible for the safety of their teams and employees are responsible for working safely and for the safety of others. In every office there are trained fire wardens (1 per 20 employees) and first aiders (1 per 30 employees). Their names are shown on the notice boards next to the lifts and on the intranet.

### 1.2 Emergencies

In case of fire, employees raise the alarm, leave the building by the nearest exit, do not use lifts, and go to the assembly point in car park A. They do not go back before the fire warden says it is safe. The emergency desk can be reached at +1 (555) 010-7788 at any time. Emergency exits are never blocked. Fire drills take place once per year in every office.

### 1.3 First aid

First aid boxes are located at reception and on every floor. In case of an accident or sudden illness, the nearest first aider is called, and the emergency services if necessary. Every case of first aid is recorded by the first aider.

### 1.4 Accidents and incidents

Every accident, near miss or unsafe situation is reported within 24 hours with form HR-210 or in PeopleHub under Report an Incident. Serious accidents that lead to hospital treatment are reported by phone to the emergency desk at once, and the company informs the authorities within the legal time. Nobody is blamed for reporting a near miss: the aim is to learn from it.

### 1.5 Ergonomics and screen work

Every employee has an adjustable chair and a height-adjustable desk or an equivalent solution. Employees take a short break of at least 10 minutes after every 2 hours of screen work. Free eye tests are described in the Employee Benefits Guide. Employees who feel pain because of their workplace tell their manager or the facilities team, who arrange an ergonomic assessment within 10 working days.

### 1.6 Special workplaces

In laboratories, warehouses and technical rooms, safety shoes, glasses and gloves are mandatory where signs say so. Employees complete the safety induction before they start work there and repeat it every 2 years. Lifting of loads over 20 kg needs two people or a lifting aid.

### 1.7 Pregnancy, disability and health conditions

Pregnant employees and employees with a disability or a long-term health condition can ask for adjustments of the workplace, tasks or hours. Facilities and People and Culture arrange a risk assessment within 10 working days after the request.

## 2. Business travel

### 2.1 Approval and booking

Business travel needs the approval of the manager before it is booked. Trips abroad that take more than 5 days need the approval of the head of department. Employees book all travel through the travel portal, which uses the company contracts and gives the company an overview in case of an emergency. Bookings outside the portal are reimbursed only with a good reason.

### 2.2 Flights

- Flights are booked in economy class.
- Employees of grade G8 and above may book business class for flights of more than 8 hours.
- Employees book at least 14 days in advance when possible, and choose the cheapest reasonable fare. Changes and cancellations are made as early as possible.
- Employees collect frequent flyer points for private use, but they do not choose a flight only for the points.

### 2.3 Hotels

Hotels are booked through the travel portal. The maximum price per night, including breakfast when it is part of the rate, depends on the city:

| City category | Maximum per night |
| Tier 1 cities (for example capitals and financial centres) | 250 USD |
| Tier 2 cities | 200 USD |
| All other places | 150 USD |

Taxes are included in the maximum. Higher prices need the approval of the head of department before the booking. The list of cities per category is published on the intranet.

### 2.4 Meals and local transport

Employees receive a daily allowance for meals of 55 USD per day of travel, which is paid without receipts. Meals that are provided by the host or included in the hotel or flight are deducted: 15 USD for breakfast, 20 USD for lunch and 20 USD for dinner. Taxis and ride services are allowed when public transport is not safe or practical. Car rental is allowed for trips of more than 2 days with the approval of the manager, in the smallest suitable category.

### 2.5 Private car

Business use of a private car is reimbursed with 0.60 USD per kilometre. The employee records the date, the route and the distance in the expense claim. Fines and parking penalties are never reimbursed.

### 2.6 Insurance and safety

The company's travel insurance covers medical costs, accidents, lost luggage and cancellations during business trips and the private days directly connected to it, up to 3 days. Employees who travel to countries with a travel warning need the approval of the Security desk. The emergency desk at +1 (555) 010-7788 helps in case of illness, accident, theft or political unrest. Employees take care of vaccinations needed for the destination, and the company pays them.

## 3. Expenses

### 3.1 How to claim

Employees submit expense claims with form HR-204 in PeopleHub within 30 days after the end of the trip or the purchase. A receipt is needed for every expense above 25 USD. The manager approves the claim within 5 working days, and Finance pays it with the next salary or within 10 working days.

### 3.2 What is reimbursed

Reimbursed are reasonable costs that were needed for the work, with a receipt: travel, hotel, local transport, visas, business phone calls and customer meals. Meals with customers are reimbursed up to 80 USD per person, and the names of the guests and the business reason are recorded.

### 3.3 What is not reimbursed

Not reimbursed are alcohol beyond a normal glass of wine or beer with dinner, personal items, minibar and entertainment in the hotel, fines, upgrades without approval, and the costs of a partner or a family member who travels along.

### 3.4 Approval limits

Managers approve expenses according to their grade: up to 500 USD for grade G3 and below, up to 1,500 USD for grades G4 to G6, up to 5,000 USD for grades G7 to G9, and up to 15,000 USD for grades G10 to G12. Claims above the limit are approved by the next higher manager. Nobody approves their own claim.

## 4. Company credit cards

Employees who travel often can ask for a company card with form HR-160. It is used only for business expenses, and receipts are uploaded in PeopleHub within 10 working days after the statement. Lost cards are blocked by calling the number on the back of the card and are reported to Finance at once.

## 5. Misuse

False claims, double claims and claims for private costs are a breach of the Code of Conduct and can lead to disciplinary action, repayment, and in serious cases dismissal.
